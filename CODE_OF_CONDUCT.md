# Community Code of Conduct

AI Creations Now wants public project discussions to remain professional, useful, and safe.

## Expected behavior

Participants should communicate respectfully, focus criticism on the technical issue, provide reproducible facts when reporting defects, respect privacy, and follow applicable laws, platform rules, and software licenses.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing private information, impersonating official releases, deceptive download links, spam, scams, harmful attachments, and repeated disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when needed to protect users, the project, or the company.

Security-sensitive matters should be reported privately through [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
