# About AI Creations Now Software Development

AI Creations Now provides independent technology consulting, custom software development, AI infrastructure assessment, and hardware/software optimization.

The company develops practical solutions around each client’s real operating requirements rather than forcing a one-size-fits-all platform. Engagements may be remote, nationwide, or international, and client details remain private unless publication is authorized.

AI Creations Now was founded in late 2020 by **Dominick Strippoli**.
